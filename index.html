Case studies

Two real negotiation situations. Supplier names and commercial details are kept confidential.

Supplier development

Winning new suppliers for a growing brand

Situation
Casper is a top 2-3 air-conditioner brand, but its newer categories (TV, refrigerator, washing machine, small appliances) are less known. Some suppliers had never worked with the company.
Action
Presented air-conditioner sales growth data to show the brand’s market potential, positioned the partnership as a long-term volume opportunity, then negotiated contracts and first orders.
Result
Opened cooperation and placed orders with suppliers that had no previous relationship with the company.

Cost negotiation

Holding unit price under price-increase pressure

Situation
Long-term suppliers requested price increases.
Action
Counter-proposed a higher MOQ in exchange for keeping the unit price, backed by consumption reports and safety-stock levels agreed with related departments. Shared consumption and quality data so both sides could balance demand.
Result
Unit price kept within safe inventory limits, ~3% saving per order through MOQ and unit-price negotiation, and a stronger long-term partnership.

Innovation & Risk control

Controlling Spec data of Artwork

Situation
Artwork-including labels, stickers, and carton that show the mainly specifications/ cautions to distributors and end-users.
Errors could occur when Spec data were omitted or entered incorrectly during design process. (especially when Designer/PIC did not have in-depth product knowledge or technical data).
Action
Utilize AI to compare the Artwork final against Spec data approved by QC department before release.
Result
Save time when comparing large data or quantities within Artwork. Minimize manual issues during the design process.

Process improvements

  1. Specifying the delivery period in the lead-time term of each PI/PO

    Lead-time of production/delivery is stated explicitly into each PI/PO at signing, giving a clear baseline for follow-up and reducing unplanned delays.

  2. Checking the production status one-week before dudate

    The supplier's production status is monitored one week prior to the end of the lead time, with the aim of avoiding potential issues and proactively preparing contingency plans in the ability.

  3. Forecasting based on monthly safety-stock report

    The over-order ratio is adjusted ahead of peak season and warranty demand to keep inventory at a safe level.
